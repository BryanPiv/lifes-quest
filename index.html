    How the savings suggestion works

    Why this amount? Fidelity suggests 10% of take-home pay for near-term goals and emergency savings. After 30 days, we cap that at half the room left after bills and logged spending. The cap is Pocket Peak’s buffer, not Fidelity’s formula.

    National picture: The Federal Reserve found 55% of U.S. adults, and 75% in households earning $100,000 or more, had three months of emergency savings in 2025. This is a comparison, not a required savings rate.
